SLO error budgets · burn-rate alerts

Turn an SLO into alerts that page for the right reasons.

Enter the SLO and the PromQL for its SLI. You get the error budget, how fast each alert fires, and rules for Prometheus, Grafana, and Terraform. The method is the multiwindow, multi-burn-rate alert from chapter 5 of the Google SRE Workbook.

Runs in your browser. No backend, no analytics, no cookies. The whole config lives in the URL, so a link is a share.

Objective

Step 1
SLI type

Each selector is a plain PromQL series selector. The tool wraps it in sum(rate(...[window])). Group-by labels go into every sum by and every and on, so each cluster or namespace burns its own budget. On Prometheus 3, whole-number bucket boundaries are stored as le="1.0", not le="1".

Alert policy

Step 2

Each row alerts when the long and the short window both burn faster than the row's rate. Burn rate = budget share × SLO window ÷ long window. The defaults are the workbook's starting point.
